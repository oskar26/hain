Rivers across the region rose to record levels on Tuesday, after three days of heavy rain, officials said.

“We have never seen the water this high,” said a shop owner, who had moved her stock upstairs during the night, together with her neighbours.

Emergency services evacuated more than 2,000 people from low-lying streets, and schools in the area stay closed until Thursday.

Forecasters expect the rain to ease on Wednesday, but warned that river levels could keep rising for another day, as water flows down from the hills.
