Wikipedia knowledge packs

The offline Wikipedia packs ("mini" ZIM files) hold texts written by the authors of
Wikipedia (https://www.wikipedia.org). They are packaged by Kiwix / openZIM
(https://www.kiwix.org) and downloaded only when you ask for them; they are not part of
the app.

The texts are licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0
International licence (CC BY-SA 4.0). Each article's history and authors can be found
on its page on Wikipedia. The full licence text follows.
